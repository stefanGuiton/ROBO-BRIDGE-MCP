## Terrain provenance

The base terrain concept image was generated with OpenAI ChatGPT image generation. Vizcom was then used to convert the concept into a 3D model. The terrain was subsequently edited, configured and integrated into ROBO BRIDGE MCP by the repository owner.

## AI-assisted development

Parts of ROBO BRIDGE MCP were developed with OpenAI/ChatGPT assistance and project-specific AI-generated implementation packages. The repository owner reviewed, modified and integrated that work. AI assistance is recorded here for transparency; it is not a separately vendored runtime dependency.
